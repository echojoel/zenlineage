# Japan and South Korea practice evidence, Batch 27

Checked: 2026-10-08  
Scope: all 13 Japan and all 14 South Korea `check_first` records in `/tmp/zen-practice-review-batch26b.csv`.

This review separates temple/sect identity, heritage, monastic training, visitor access, temple stays or other visitor programs, and recurring public Zen practice. A temple's Zen/Seon affiliation or monastic training does not by itself establish lay access. A heritage listing or a temple stay is visitor evidence, not a recurring public meditation schedule. Missing evidence is uncertainty, not misconduct or inactivity.
