# France practice evidence, Batch 24 (2026-10-08)

## Primary network evidence

The French-speaking Plum Village sangha directory updated 8 June 2025 is the item-level source used for this batch:

- https://sanghasfrancophonespruniers.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/liste_sangha_franco-08-06-2025.pdf

It names local sanghas and departments/places, includes contact information for many entries, and links several regional sangha pages. It is a network roster, not a public schedule or venue directory. A roster entry supports an organization identity and regional footprint only. In particular, it marks the Vannes and Poitiers sanghas “SANGHA EN PAUSE”; they were removed from the current-practice map while paused. This is a status decision, not a claim that either sangha has closed.
