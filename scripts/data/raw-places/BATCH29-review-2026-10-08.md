# Batch 29 European practice review (2026-10-08)

Reviewed the 36 western European records selected from `/tmp/zen-practice-review-batch29.csv` for France, Germany, Italy, Spain, the United Kingdom, Greece, Portugal, Switzerland, the Netherlands, and Turkey. The set comprises 33 `check_first` records plus three routine secondary-source records (Roanne, TAO Bamberg, and Ravensburg). Three French entries reside in `zen-places-fr-gap.json`, not `zen-places-fr.json`.

The review used current first-party and institutional pages where available, with current institutional/directories and dated notices as secondary evidence. Archived pages and old directory records were context only. Lack of a current page or timetable was treated as uncertainty, not evidence of closure or misconduct. No personal email addresses are reproduced here.

## Removed from the current-practice map

- **Groupe Zen de Guadeloupe (Le Gosier), Méditations Zen Sōtō Marseille (Uchiyama), and Sé-un Zendo (Cambous)** — these were only found in the old, third-party bouddha.ch France list. Targeted current searches found no item-level first-party or institutional evidence confirming the claimed entities, current public venues, or recurring practice. The stale directory entries would make the current map imply a verified place and schedule that could not be substantiated. Their removal is a listing-evidence decision, not a closure or conduct finding.
- **Casa Zen – Scuola delle Quattro Foglie (Gazzuolo)** — the current official kyudo.net site identifies the school’s Heki Ryu Insai Ha kyūdō teaching and ordinary Saturday archery practice. The older CESNUR description also records historical Zen activity, but no current Zen practice programme was found. Keeping it as a current Zen practice place would misdescribe what its present official site supports.
- **Stella del Mattino – Gruppo zazen di Fano, Gruppo zazen di Livorno, and Zazen a Pescara** — the current official pages say Fano’s venue is closed, Livorno’s former venue is unavailable and participants sit at home while seeking space, and Pescara’s former Centro Lokanatha venue is no longer available while a new one is sought. These pages do not establish a current public venue for a local map pin. This does not establish that the groups dissolved.
- **Tao’s Center (Paros)** — its current official site describes a seasonal retreat and wellness center with mixed meditation, movement, yoga, and other programmes, including 2026 activities. It does not identify the center as a Zen organization or establish a current Zen practice group. This removes a venue outside the substantiated Zen-specific scope; it is not a finding about the center’s activities.
- **Plum Village Swiss Inter-Sangha (Lausanne)** — current descriptions characterize this as a Switzerland-wide community-building initiative; the surfaced event material is historical and does not establish an ongoing local Lausanne practice venue. Mapping the national network to Lausanne would imply a local group and venue that were not established. This is a map-scope correction, not evidence the network has ceased.
- **Cem Şen Sangha (İstanbul)** — the current Cem Şen site presents an education and teaching programme, while targeted official-source checks did not identify a distinct Zen sangha, public Zen practice timetable, or fixed practice venue. The prior listing as a public Zen sangha at city level is unsupported. This is an identity/scope decision, not a conduct finding.
